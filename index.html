Sanad QR Reader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Overview

Sanad QR Reader is an internal browser extension that allows authorized users to select QR codes displayed on webpages, decode them, and view the extracted data.

Data Processing

When a user initiates QR selection, the extension temporarily captures the visible browser tab to crop the selected QR code. QR decoding and display of the extracted data take place locally in the browser.

Data Collection and Sharing

The extension does not intentionally store screenshots or QR data persistently, and does not transmit them to external servers. It does not sell user data or use it for advertising.

Purpose

The extension is intended solely to help authorized company users read and review QR-encoded document data. It does not verify document authenticity.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact your organization's designated extension administrator.
